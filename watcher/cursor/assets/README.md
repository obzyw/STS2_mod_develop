# 观者光标素材来源

`watcher-cookie-v1.png` 使用内置 image_gen 生成，透明背景。参考用户本机 CookieCursor 的 necrobinder/icon.png、silent/icon.png 仅匹配饼干材质/描边风格；使用本机无保存观者画面仅识别观者形象。第三方参考图不随包分发。生成的源图原样保存，只有游戏运行时进行纹理导入缩放和作者原有倾斜处理。

模式：内置工具；不是 CLI/API 回退。完整提示词：

Reference 3 is IDENTITY ONLY: the small blue-grey haired purple-clothed woman standing on the LEFT of the screenshot is the Watcher the user plays. Ignore ALL other things in the screenshot. Give her short navy/slate-blue hair with side-swept bangs, a rounded high bun at upper-left/back, TWO thin GOLD hair sticks in the bun, fair skin, gentle peacefully CLOSED thin black eyes, subtle pink cheek blush, a tiny calm knowing smile, just a small purple and magenta scarf/collar at the bottom. Head only, no body, no hands, no staff. Face frontal with a subtle three-quarter turn toward viewer right. Do not give her Necrobinder's white hair, hat, skull, spiral ornament, horns or stitches. Make the face and hair large enough to read at 64x64 pixels. Fit the full outer black cookie outline and all hairpins within a square with a small even transparent margin. Entire background truly alpha transparent, no white or checkerboard backdrop, no text, no watermark, no UI, no separate arrow, no glow, no extra objects. Export a single square icon.
